AreaTrek Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026
AreaTrek ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information (name, email, phone number)
  • Identity Verification Data (government ID, selfie verification)
  • Profile Information (photos, languages, interests, location)
  • Booking Information (trip details, payment records)
  • In-app communications and messages
  • Device & usage data (IP address, logs, analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify identity and enhance platform safety
  • Match travelers with local guides
  • Process bookings and payments
  • Improve user experience and app performance
  • Provide customer support

3. Sharing of Information

We never sell your data. We only share information when necessary:

  • Between travelers and guides for bookings
  • With payment and verification partners
  • When required by law

4. Data Security

We use encryption, secure servers, and strict access controls to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.

5. Location Data

Location data is used for nearby searches, bookings, and safety features like trip tracking. You can control this anytime from your device settings.

6. Safety Features

  • Verified guide system
  • Live GPS tracking
  • SOS emergency support
  • Report & block users
  • 24/7 customer support

7. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Update or correct information
  • Request account deletion
  • Disable location tracking

8. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as required for legal, operational, and safety purposes.

9. Third-Party Services

We use third-party tools for payments, analytics, and verification. These providers follow their own privacy policies.

10. Children’s Privacy

AreaTrek is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant updates will be notified within the app.
